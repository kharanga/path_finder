A one-line strip of session chips (a ring on the dot means working, an orange count means inbox), and a toast for each message that fades after it is read. The window can shrink to the strip alone when nothing is in flight.

Best for
The smallest footprint, like a menu-bar or corner widget.
Loses
Anything older than the last two or three messages.

Combined: strip, active pair, fading history

D's strip stays at the top as the always-on status line. B's two cards take the middle whenever a message is in flight, and swap to whichever pair is talking. Under them, D's toasts become a short history that fades out, newest first. When nothing moves, the pod stays up showing the last message, with its age in the caption; only the history fades.

Combined, with several messages in flight

The pod always shows the newest message with the two full cards. Every other message in flight gets a compact row under it, newest first, capped at two, then a count. A session talking to two others appears in the pod and in a row with the same status. When a compact row's message is read, it drops into the fading history.

Web: every session a node, every message an arrow

All sessions are laid out in a ring, each as the card from your frames. A message is an arrow from sender to receiver with its summary and stage at the midpoint. Only messages in flight are arrows: Pending grey, In inbox green, Failed red. A Read message flashes its double check for three seconds and leaves; the newest one is shown mid-flash here. Idle notices are not arrows; they pulse the eye glyph on the watching node. With two sessions the same layout is just the pod.

arrowsOne per message, sender to receiver, label at the midpoint: summary and stage. Two sessions talking both ways get two parallel arrows.

webRing of cards; arrows only for Pending, In inbox, and Failed, plus a three-second Read flash. One arrow per pair, with a count when several are in flight.
stripOne chip per session. Ring on the dot while working, orange count while mail waits. Clicking a chip highlights that session's arrows in the web.
toastsOne per message in flight, newest first, with the full first line of the message. The left edge is green for In inbox and Read, grey for Pending, red for Failed.
fadingA toast dims when its message is read and leaves after about eight seconds, so the list is usually two or three deep.
hoverHovering a toast lights up its arrow; hovering an arrow lifts its toast. Same message, two handles.
heightWeb 400, strip 40, three toasts about 170: roughly 640 pixels tall at 380 wide. The toast list is the part that scrolls if it ever grows.

Ten sessions

Past eight sessions the small window switches to compact nodes: dot, name, inbox count, and the working ring, with the full card on hover. Labels show on hover; the toasts carry the text. Widen the window and the full cards come back.

thresholdCompact nodes when the ring cannot hold full cards without overlap: more than eight sessions at 380 wide, more than fourteen at 680.
labelsCompact mode labels only the newest arrow. Hovering any arrow or toast shows its label; the toasts carry the text anyway.
foldersBy default the window shows one folder, as the --root flag does now. "All folders" is a switch in the title bar; sessions from other folders get a muted dot ring.
unrelated trafficSessions with no messages in the last ten minutes sink to the strip only and leave the ring, so the web stays about the sessions that talk.

All of these read the same /state and /events feed the current page uses, so any of them is a new page under viz/ rather than a watcher change. For a real popup window later, the same page can be opened with a fixed small size by the browser, or wrapped in a small native shell; the data path does not change.
